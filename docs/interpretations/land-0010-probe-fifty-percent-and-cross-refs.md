# land-0010 — Probe 50% test and stale cross-references in Close Assault

- **Cases:** land:15.25, land:15.91, land:15.13, land:15.12
- **Status:** adopted
- **Profile version:** v1
- **Decided by:** rules-land, 2026-10-06
- **Owner review:** reviewed 2026-10-06 by the owner (batch review 1)

## Question
(1) What does "available TOE Strength Points" mean for the 50% test that turns an assault into a Probe, and is the test per hex or across all assaults the units make? (2) 15.13 and 15.12 cite Overrun in 15.86, which is the captured-points case; the Overrun loss rule is 15.84.

## Evidence
15.25 and 15.91 refer to the units' available points "in that, or another, attack". The 15.25 example (TOE 7: 2 against hex A, 1 against hex B, 4 withheld for ammunition: both are Probes; 3 against A makes both full assaults) shows the test is on the total committed across all assaults and that withheld points still count as available.

## Ruling
The units making the assault(s) are tested together: if the total TOE points they commit across all assaults in the segment is at least 50% of the TOE points of the participating units (including points withheld for any reason), every one of those assaults is a full Close Assault; otherwise each is a Probe. References to Overrun losses in 15.12/15.13 are read as 15.84.

## Rationale
Matches the worked example in 15.25 and the wording "that or another attack" in 15.91.

## Affected behaviour and tests
Probe classification. Test: the example from 15.25 in both variants. Test: an ammo-starved unit with all points withheld is a Probe.
