## Question
What body strength should fuel and activity-water billing use for the Commonwealth cw.a headquarters whose class maximum is not applicable, despite an OA Normal marker or absent TOE?

## Evidence
The land:4.46 chart a class chart marks the cw.a maximum as not applicable. The bound class record therefore has no numeric maximum. Eighteen current OA headquarters use this class: seventeen have a Normal N field and one omits TOE. A missing numeric maximum alone cannot distinguish this cited chart fact from an unresolved source. Units-0005 and units-0006 cover the separate nine headquarters with numeric, unparenthesized maxima.

## Provisional ruling
The cited cw.a headquarters body has zero TOE-strength points for both Normal N and absent TOE. It therefore needs no body movement fuel under airlog:49.12/49.13 and no body activity water under airlog:52.42. First-line trucks retain their canonical fuel, tank-capacity and activity-water accounting, including heat and the per-OpStage ledger. This does not invent a maximum or grant free truck movement.

Bind the exception to the known cw.a headquarters class, not every missing maximum. Explicit weapon lists keep their equipment billing. Under/Over and invalid or unresolved source inputs retain their canonical checks. Men-only parenthesized HQs and the numeric-HQ house rules remain unchanged. No shared schema, equipment entitlement or new ledger field is added.

## Affected behaviour and tests
Canonical strength and the existing HQ billing resolver carry this source distinction to movement fuel, innate fuel capacity, activity water and public-content water preflight. Controls cover all eighteen authored records, the actual Normal/absent distribution, zero body charges, separate truck fuel/capacity/water, heat, shortage purity and stage idempotence. A deliberately omitted numeric-class maximum retains missing-source refusal and privacy controls. Movement coverage requires an actual cw.a HQ to complete an ordinary move in both profiles with its truck demands supplied. Both unchanged campaign functional tests remain required before publication.
