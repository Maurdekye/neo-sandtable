# land-0033 - Minefield clearance requires an idle engineering unit

- **Cases:** land:23.22, land:24.38, land:26.13
- **Status:** adopted
- **Profile version:** v1
- **Decided by:** rules-engineers, 2026-10-07
- **Owner review:** reviewed 2026-10-07 by the owner (batch review 3)

## Question
Can an engineering unit clear a minefield during a stage in which it remains stationary but spends CP on an action other than movement?

## Evidence
Case 23.22 makes clearance conditional on a whole stage in the enemy field without any CP expenditure. Case 24.38 extends clearance to friendly real fields and refers back to 23.22. Case 26.13 requires presence at both boundaries and excludes movement CP, without expressly excluding other CP. Engineering HQs can have other activities, so the difference matters.

## Ruling
Require the same eligible engineering unit to remain in the real minefield hex throughout the complete Operations Stage and to spend zero CP during that stage, for friendly and enemy fields. Check a stage activity ledger before CP resets; a departure or any expenditure prevents clearance even if a later refund restores the net CP balance to zero. An arrival after the opening boundary does not qualify.

## Rationale
This applies the unqualified expenditure restriction in 23.22 and its cross-reference in 24.38 consistently to both owners. The movement restriction in 26.13 remains satisfied. Allowing other CP activity would depart from 23.22's explicit condition.

## Affected behaviour and tests
The engineering stage-boundary ledger captures original location, departure and positive CP expenditure before resets. Planned tests cover stationary clearance, mid-stage entry, leave-and-return, non-movement CP, refunds, friendly versus enemy fields, and an ineligible or eliminated builder. These tests are planned; no procedure has landed yet.
