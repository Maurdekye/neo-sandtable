# 0012 — A real friendly dump permits ammunition-free enemy-ZOC entry

- **Cases:** airlog:50.12
- **Status:** adopted
- **Profile version:** cna-2021-dev and cna-2021-full
- **Decided by:** neo-sandtable, 2026-10-07
- **Owner review:** pending (consequential; batch 2)

## Question

Does a friendly dump need ammunition to permit an ammunition-free unit to enter an enemy-controlled hex?

## Evidence

Airlog:50.12 gives a stocked friendly unit and a friendly supply dump as separate alternatives. The ammunition qualification belongs to the friendly unit alternative. A dummy is a deception marker rather than a real supply dump.

## Ruling

An ammunition-free unit may enter an enemy-controlled hex when accompanied by a friendly unit with ammunition, or when entering a hex occupied by a real friendly supply dump, regardless of the dump's current contents. Dummy dumps do not qualify. This permission provides neither a ZOC nor combat strength to the dry unit and does not waive normal cargo or ammunition consumption rules.

## Rationale

This follows the separate alternatives literally and avoids making dummy contents observable through order legality.

## Affected behaviour and tests

The authoritative movement control check must distinguish stocked friendly units, real empty friendly dumps and dummy dumps, retaining exclusions for moving units when testing arrival conditions. Planning continues to use only disclosed enemy control information.
