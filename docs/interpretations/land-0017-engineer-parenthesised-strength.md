# land-0017 — Engineers and parenthesised strengths (23.11)

- **Cases:** land:23.11, land:3.4
- **Status:** proposed
- **Profile version:** v1
- **Decided by:** rules-land, 2026-10-06
- **Owner review:** pending

## Question
23.11 first lets Engineers use their parenthesised strength only when sharing a hex with a friendly combat unit, then a "correction and clarification" reverses this: the rating applies only when no friendly combat unit is present.

## Evidence
The correction matches the general rule for parenthesised ratings in 3.4 and 15.17: parenthesised ratings are usable only in a hex with no combat units of the owner.

## Ruling
Engineers use parenthesised strengths only when not stacked with a friendly combat unit; they may always enter friendly-occupied enemy-controlled hexes.

## Rationale
The correction agrees with the general parenthesised-strength rule.

## Affected behaviour and tests
Defence strength of engineers. Test: an engineer company alone in a hex defends with its parenthesised rating; stacked with an infantry battalion it adds nothing.
