# land-0027: Sandstorm exposure in breakdown checks

**Status:** proposed
**Cases:** land:21.37, land:21.38, land:29.51

## Question

The procedure in 21.37(d) measures sandstorm exposure as a share of movement CP. The note printed under chart 21.38 instead measures the share of accumulated breakdown points. Those measures can give different column shifts on mixed terrain.

## Proposed reading

Track movement CP spent in sections experiencing sandstorms and total movement CP for each movement, reaction or retreat window. Apply the sandstorm shift when the former is at least half the latter. Breaking-off and detachment CP do not describe travel through a map section and do not enter either total. Keep the chart's printed note unchanged in the transcribed data and flag the discrepancy there.

## Reason

Case 21.37 defines how to measure exposure during movement; the chart supplies the percentages after the column has been chosen. This is a procedural disagreement rather than a disagreement about a chart value. In land-0018 the construction chart governs its numeric values, which does not settle the separate question of how this procedure counts weather exposure.

## Consequences

Two routes with identical breakdown-point totals can receive different weather shifts if their CP exposure differs. A mixed route that spends half its travel CP in storm sections receives the shift even when those sections contributed less than half its breakdown points.
