# Cartographer corridor surface review 0001

122 previously unclassified cells in the retained C/western-D share were
individually inspected on eight source-native 2x sheets. Sixteen boundary
and shoreline cells were enlarged to 6x. The batch accepts 121 new cells:
105 clear, nine rough and seven sea. Six clear cells are coastal.

The rough cells are C3528,C3628,C3728,C3729,C3730,C3731,C3828,C3831,C3832.
The sea cells are C4128,C4228,C4229,C4230,C4231,C4232,C4233.
Coastal clear is C4028,C4129,C4130,C4131,C4132,C4133.
The full accepted ID list and decisions are in reviews/map-cartographer-0001.toml.

Enlarged C3732,C3733,C3833 show edge-aligned brown contour bands with white
substrate; these do not become rough terrain. Beige patches outside
C3829,C3830,C3928,C3929,C3931,C3932,C3933 remain neighboring terrain.
C4128's southeast shore fragment is outside its full shared side, so this
cell remains entirely water. C4026 retains its previously reviewed coastal
flag and unreadable substrate: the tiny visible fragment is insufficient
to diagnose its class reliably. No old review is overwritten.

Point symbols at Bir Khamsa, Bir Emba, Sidi Barrani and Bir el Qatrani are
observations for later place verification, not new facilities in this batch.
Line and hexside evidence is owned by map-lines and unchanged here.
Do not infer feature absence from these surface records.

Every published surface decision is directly inspected against the 2021
map and TEC.png. No classifier label, random error bound, independent
agreement or original1979 equivalence is claimed. Post-review population
error remains unmeasured. Source images and crops stay outside the repo.
